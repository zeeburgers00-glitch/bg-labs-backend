YOUR ACCOUNT

Sign in to ViMax

Your ViMax account keeps your chat and connections private. Google sign-in identifies your account; each channel needs separate permission.

New accounts need email confirmation. Delivery may be limited until the deployment owner configures email. Never share passwords or keys in chat.

Each service requires its own consent. Connections do not give chat access to other websites.

MAKE IT YOURS

Chat settings

Prompts and replies are saved to your account until deleted. The selected provider receives the current prompt and up to three previous turns. A fallback may send them to another provider. Calls may cost money; 10 attempts per account and 50 shared attempts per UTC day are limits, not spending caps.

Voice availability depends on your browser and device. Browser speech services may process your audio or text.

YOUR CHANNEL

Manage channels

Connect each YouTube channel separately. You can save up to ten and switch between them. Chat cannot read or publish to channels yet.

Checking channel setup…

Saved access can expire or be revoked. Disconnect asks Google to revoke the grant; other channels using the same grant may also need to reconnect.

YOUR CONNECTIONS

Facebook Pages

Facebook requires its own login and permission; Google sign-in does not grant access. These connections list Pages only—chat cannot read or post to Facebook yet.

Checking Facebook setup…

Removing one Page from ViMax does not revoke the Facebook app grant. Review access in Facebook settings.
